## 1. Academic Computer Vision & Agronomic Datasets

1. **PlantVillage Dataset:**  
   *Citation:* Hughes, D. P., & Salathé, M. (2015). *An open access repository of images on plant health to enable the development of mobile disease diagnostics.* arXiv preprint arXiv:1511.08060.  
   *Relevance:* Foundational benchmark containing 54,306 laboratory-captured images across 14 crop species and 26 diseases.

2. **PlantDoc Dataset (In-Field Computer Vision):**  
   *Citation:* Singh, A. K., Ganapathysubramanian, B., Sarkar, S., & Singh, A. (2020). *Deep learning for plant disease detection in real-world agricultural environments.* Frontiers in Plant Science, 11, 574369.  
   *Relevance:* 2,598 real-world in-field images addressing complex backgrounds, illumination variations, and occlusions.

3. **CropPest & Pest24 Visual Benchmarks:**  
   *Citation:* Wang, Q., et al. (2020). *Pest24: A large-scale multi-target dataset for agricultural pest detection in the wild.* Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, 175, 105560.

## 2. Neural Architecture & Mobile Deep Learning

4. **MobileNetV3 (Hardware-Aware Search):**  
   *Citation:* Howard, A., Sandler, M., Chu, G., Chen, L. C., Chen, B., Tan, M., Wang, W., Zhu, Y., Pang, R., Vasudevan, V., Le, Q. V., & Adam, H. (2019). *Searching for MobileNetV3.* Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 1314-1324.  
   *Relevance:* Primary recommended backbone for lightweight edge vision with Squeeze-and-Excitation attention blocks.

5. **EfficientNet & EfficientNet-Lite:**  
   *Citation:* Tan, M., & Le, Q. (2019). *EfficientNet: Rethinking model scaling for convolutional neural networks.* International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 6105-6114.

6. **On-Device Calibration & Out-of-Distribution Detection (Future Reference):**  
   *Citation:* Guo, C., Pleiss, G., Sun, Y., & Weinberger, K. Q. (2017). *On calibration of modern neural networks.* International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 1321-1330.  
   *Relevance:* Theoretical basis for temperature scaling in future validated ML pipeline iterations.
